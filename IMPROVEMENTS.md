# Website review and improvements

Reviewed 24 October 2026 from the public website. These are design and content observations, not a measured performance, accessibility or SEO audit.

## Implemented in this redesign

1. **Bring vehicles closer to the first screen.** The original home page introduces the business, marque logos and videos before inventory. The redesign moves the collection immediately after the hero and a compact marque strip.
2. **Make browsing useful.** Add make/model search, category filters, price sorting, year sorting, clear prices and an empty-results recovery action.
3. **Create a calmer premium presentation.** Keep the existing black and gold identity with a restrained olive undertone, consistent typography, more spacing, larger photography and a clear visual hierarchy. A light process section gives the long page a change in pace.
4. **Reduce enquiry friction.** Add vehicle-specific WhatsApp drafts, a viewing CTA, mobile contact access and directions. Avoid a contact form that appears to send leads without a working backend.
5. **Make details comparable.** Use consistent engine, mileage, year and category information; explicitly mark unknown details rather than inventing them.
6. **Keep sold stock out of the selected collection.** The source homepage includes sold cars among current listings. The concept only uses vehicles not marked sold at review time, while reminding visitors to confirm availability.
7. **Explain finance assumptions.** Put an illustrative calculator on the page instead of requiring visitors to leave it. Make the selected rate editable and distinguish an illustration from a lender offer.
8. **Support mobile and keyboard visitors.** Responsive grids, a collapsible navigation menu, visible focus, skip navigation, reduced motion, labeled inputs, a native modal and live result counts.

## Before production launch

- Resolve the BMW 420i year mismatch: the title and description say 2023; one specification field says 2021.
- Verify every listing, current price, availability, photo permission, vehicle specification, business detail and opening hour.
- Connect an inventory CMS or feed so sold status and pricing update in one place. Add stock IDs, full photo galleries, service records and inspection documentation when supplied by the business.
- Add verified customer reviews only with permission and a traceable source. Do not publish invented ratings or testimonials.
- Confirm which financing calculation methods and disclosures apply with the business's lenders before making financing claims.
- Add dedicated crawlable vehicle pages, canonical metadata and verified structured data after the final hosting/domain plan is selected. Modal details in this concept are not dedicated SEO landing pages.
- Set up analytics and conversion events with appropriate consent, then measure search use, WhatsApp clicks, viewing enquiries and actual sales.
- Run performance and accessibility audits on the production host. Compress oversized images further and use responsive image variants as the photo catalogue grows.
- Plan the existing NEWPAGES migration and redirects before moving the domain. Keep original product URLs mapped to their replacements.
